Architectural Verification & Ingestion Principles

1. Stacked Separation

Stacked multi-season CSVs are parsed row-by-row, grouped by normalized season identity, and ingested as strictly isolated season-specific batches.

2. Zero Season Guesswork

Calendar seasons (e.g. 2025) are preserved and never coerced into European seasons (2025/2026). Match dates are validated against competition season boundaries.

3. Idempotent Deduplication

Deterministic natural match keys (Competition_Season_Date_Home_Away) and source record hashing guarantee zero duplicate matches upon re-import.

4. Full Provenance Traceability

Every imported match and quarantine row retains its physical source file name, row number, and raw source values for auditing and one-click correction.
